Progressive difficulty

Cards unlock as you learn

You never see a hard card type before you've demonstrated the fundamentals. The deck watches your review intervals and introduces harder directions only when you're ready.

EN → DE · Production

Active from day 0

See the English word, produce the German. Your entry point for every new word.

DE → EN · Recognition

Unlocks at 14-day interval

Once you can reliably produce the word, test whether you can recognise it in German.

Sentence Cloze · Context

Unlocks at 21-day interval (both directions)

Read a German sentence with the word blanked out. Can you fill it in from context?

Listening · Auditory

Unlocks at 21-day interval

Hear the word spoken. No text. Can you identify it from sound alone?

What makes this different

Not another frequency list

📖

Built from real texts

Vocabulary extracted from German literature and professional domains using NLP. Words come with authentic sentence context from the source material, not synthetic textbook examples.

🔊

Pronunciation included

IPA transcriptions sourced from German Wiktionary. Audio files where available. Words Wiktionary doesn't cover get LLM-generated IPA as a fallback.

🔄

Disambiguation built in

When two German words share the same English translation — essen and fressen both mean “to eat” — each card carries a quiet hint distinguishing its sense and usage.

🌙

Dark & light mode

Cards follow your system appearance. Responsive typography scales from phone to desktop via CSS clamp(). Tested on Anki desktop and AnkiMobile.

💬

Grammar hints on tap

Cloze cards have a hidden grammar hint (case, article, verb form) that appears when you tap the blank. Zero visual clutter until you need the scaffold.

⚙️

Seed it with your own reading

Drop in a chapter of whatever you're reading in German — a novel, a news article, a transcript. The CLI extracts vocabulary, skips words you already know, and generates complete Anki cards.

Bonus deck

Grammar terms in context

A companion sub-deck for grammar terminology — cases, tenses, moods, clause types. Two card directions: see the term and recall its definition, or see an example and identify the grammar at work. These connect to the cloze hints on vocab cards, so when a cloze blank says “Dativ · maskulin,” you already know what that means.

Toolkit

Bring your own German text

The real power is feeding it text you're actually reading. Drop in a chapter of a novel, a news article, or a transcript — the CLI extracts vocabulary with NLP, skips words you already know, generates card fields with an LLM, and imports them into Anki. Your reading becomes your study material.

# You're reading Schachnovelle — turn chapters 1-5 into cards anki-german generate text \ --file data/books/Schachnovelle.txt \ --source schachnovelle --paragraphs 1-120 # Enrich with IPA and audio from Wiktionary anki-german enrich audio # Push latest card designs to Anki anki-german templates # Unsuspend the next batch of ready cards anki-german unsuspend --apply

Get started

What you need

To use the deck

Anki is free on desktop, free on Android (AnkiDroid), and free on the web (AnkiWeb). The iOS app is paid. You'll also need the AnkiConnect add-on for the desktop app.

To generate new cards

Python 3.13+ and uv for dependency management. Clone the repo, run uv sync, and the anki-german CLI is ready.

FSRS recommended

The deck works with Anki's FSRS scheduler. Set Desired Retention to 0.85 in deck options for optimal spacing.
